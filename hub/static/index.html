Routines

Automatic routines use live weather and the sun: lights come on when it actually gets dark (earlier when it's cloudy) and the mini-splits pick heating, cooling or drying on their own. Lighting curves keep lights on a gentle schedule (dimmer and warmer as the evening goes on). Routines run steps when a time arrives, the sun rises or sets, the weather changes, or a device changes. A routine with no trigger is a scene you run by hand.
